# DIALED IN — Design Language v1.0

> Deep focus. Zero jitters. No crash.

## 1. Concept

**"Precision. Tuned. Locked on."**

The brand metaphor is a **dial** — a focus ring with a single notch, like tuning
a radio to the exact frequency. Being "dialed in" is finding the precise setting
where the noise falls away and the work flows.

Everything visual hangs off this one idea:
- The **dial mark** (logo icon) = concentric focus ring + signal notch
- The **wordmark** = tight, technical, all-caps
- The **palette** = near-black ink + one signal accent (nothing else screams)

## 2. Logo

### Dial Mark (icon)
Concentric rings. A thin ink ring on the outside, a bold **signal** arc inside,
one notch marker at 45° — the "locked on" position. A single dot at center.

### Wordmark
`DIALED IN` in **Space Grotesk Bold**, all caps, tracking tight (-1%).
"DIALED" in ink, "IN" in signal — or full ink with the dial mark leading.

### Lockups
1. **Mark + wordmark** (primary) — dial mark left, `DIALED IN` right
2. **Wordmark only** (compact)
3. **Mark only** (favicon / app icon / product top)

## 3. Color

| Token | Hex | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|
| `ink` | `#0C0F0D` | Headlines, primary text, dark surfaces |
| `paper` | `#FFFFFF` | Backgrounds, light surfaces |
| `fog` | `#F3F5F3` | Secondary backgrounds, sections |
| `line` | `#E4E8E5` | Borders, dividers |
| `signal` | `#A3E635` | THE accent — CTAs, dial notch, highlights |
| `signal-ink` | `#4D7A0F` | Accent on light backgrounds (readable text) |
| `muted` | `#5F6B63` | Secondary text, captions |

**Rule:** ink + paper do 90% of the work. `signal` appears sparingly — one
notch, one CTA, one highlight per view. It's a laser, not a floodlight.

## 4. Typography

| Role | Font | Weight | Notes |
|------|------|--------|-------|
| Display / wordmark | **Space Grotesk** | 500–700 | Technical, geometric, distinctive |
| Body | **Inter** | 400–600 | Clean, readable |
| Data / labels | **Space Mono** | 400 | Clinical — doses, "60 caps", batch codes |

**Scale (mobile → desktop):**
- H1 (hero): 44 → 84px, tracking -3%
- H2 (section): 30 → 44px, tracking -1.5%
- H3 (card): 20px
- Body: 15–17px
- Label / mono: 12–13px

## 5. Visual motifs

1. **The dial** — concentric rings. Used as: logo, favicon, section dividers,
   loading spinners, product-packaging top.
2. **The notch** — a 45° accent wedge. The single "signal" moment in any layout.
3. **Grid lines** — faint technical crosshair/registration marks in backgrounds.
4. **Rounded-square cards** (14px radius) + 999px pill CTAs.

## 6. Packaging direction

- **Format:** matte dark cylinder (like premium skincare) or rigid box
- **Finish:** matte black/ink base, **embossed** `DIALED IN` wordmark
- **Accent:** a single `signal` band per product — the product name sits on it
- **Product naming:** `DIALED IN — FOCUS`, future `— SLEEP`, `— ENERGY`
- **Clinical strip:** mono type, "60 CAPSULES · 30 SERVINGS · THIRD-PARTY TESTED"

### Hero formula — DIALED IN · FOCUS
- Lion's Mane — nerve growth + cognitive clarity

- L-Theanine — calm focus, no jitters
- Alpha-GPC — choline for memory + drive
- Vitamin D3 (3000 IU) + K2 — mood, brain + bone support

- Magnesium — calm + recovery, keeps the jitters away

## 7. Voice

Confident, no-nonsense, first-person. Built by someone who actually takes it.
No "unlock your potential" fluff. Short sentences. Effect first.

## 8. Do / Don't

**Do:** ink + paper + one signal accent. Tight type. The dial motif. Clean whitespace.
**Don't:** neon-everywhere, gradient overload, stock fitness photos, ingredient jargon upfront, anything that looks like an energy drink.
