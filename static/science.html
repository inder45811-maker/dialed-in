The science, simplified.

No magic. No hacks. Just ingredients with real research behind them, at doses that actually do something.

COGNITION

Lion's Mane

Stimulates nerve growth factor (NGF), supporting neurogenesis — the growth of new brain cells. Linked to improved memory and cognitive clarity.

ENERGY

Cordyceps

Increases ATP production — your cells' energy currency — for steady, crash-free endurance without the stimulant spike.

CALM FOCUS

L-Theanine

Promotes alpha brain waves — the relaxed-alertness state. Smooths out stimulation for focus without jitters or anxiety.

MEMORY

Alpha-GPC

A highly bioavailable choline source and acetylcholine precursor — the neurotransmitter behind memory, learning, and mental drive.

MOOD + BONE

Vitamin D3 + K2

D3 supports mood, cognition, and immunity (vitamin D receptors are everywhere in the brain). K2 directs calcium to bones where it belongs.

NEUROTRANSMITTERS

Zinc

Essential for neurotransmitter function, healthy testosterone, and immune response. Most people are running low.

RECOVERY

Magnesium (glycinate)

Involved in 300+ enzyme reactions — muscle recovery, nerve function, and relaxation. The glycinate form is gentle and highly absorbed.
